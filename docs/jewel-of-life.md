# Jewel of Life

Jewel of Life has a **70% success chance per eligible use**, both when adding an
additional item option and when increasing an existing option by one level.
Each attempt is random; 70% does not guarantee seven successes in ten attempts.

On failure, the existing additional option is removed. An eligible attempt
consumes the jewel whether it succeeds or fails. Items which cannot receive
that option, or whose option is already at its allowed maximum, are not
upgraded and do not consume the jewel.

This rule is enforced by the server. A client update is not required.
