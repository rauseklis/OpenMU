# Blood Castle rewards

Blood Castle +1 through +7 give fixed EXP rewards. They do not scale with character level or the server's x5/x10 monster EXP rate.

| BC | Gate EXP | Statue EXP | Successful completion EXP | Total before time bonus and monster EXP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| +1 | 45,000 | 45,000 | 70,000 | 160,000 |
| +2 | 140,000 | 140,000 | 210,000 | 490,000 |
| +3 | 260,000 | 260,000 | 370,000 | 890,000 |
| +4 | 390,000 | 390,000 | 510,000 | 1,290,000 |
| +5 | 500,000 | 500,000 | 590,000 | 1,590,000 |
| +6 | 2,210,000 | 2,210,000 | 2,230,000 | 6,650,000 |
| +7 | 5,300,000 | 5,300,000 | 3,500,000 | 14,100,000 |

Gate and statue EXP are awarded at the end to players remaining for the payout when those objectives were destroyed; the final hit and party membership do not restrict these objective bonuses. Completion EXP is for the winner and winning-party members. The total column assumes the player qualifies for all three rewards.

The original remaining-time bonus stays unchanged: +1 gives 160 EXP per remaining second, +2 gives 180, +3 gives 200, +4 gives 220, +5 gives 240, +6 gives 260, and +7 gives 280. This is extra fixed EXP. Monster EXP is separate and uses normal server EXP settings.

For example, BC +7 with 30 seconds remaining gives an eligible winner 14,100,000 + 8,400 = **14,108,400 EXP**, plus monster EXP. The 14,100,000 base total is about 39.91% of level 355 to 356, or 23.00% of level 390 to 391 under the default EXP formula.

## Item and Zen rewards

These require the surviving winner or surviving members of the winning party.

| BC | Reward |
|---|---|
| +1 | 1,000,000 Zen and 1 random jewel |
| +2 | 2,000,000 Zen and 2 random jewels |
| +3 | 3,000,000 Zen and 3 random jewels |
| +4 | 4,000,000 Zen and 4 random jewels |
| +5 | 1 Green Ribbon Box |
| +6 | 1 Blue Ribbon Box |
| +7 | 1 Red Ribbon Box |

Each jewel draw has equal 25% chances for Bless, Soul, Life and Chaos; duplicates are possible. Item rewards enter the inventory or drop on the ground if there is no inventory space.

BC +8, score rules, Ribbon opening contents and odds, Devil Square rewards, and monster EXP settings are unchanged. BC score is event points, not a spendable currency.

The update restores empty standard BC +5 through +7 Ribbon payout pools and protects event reward groups from ordinary bonus-source cleanup. It runs once; later restarts preserve administrator edits. No client update is required: reconnect after deployment.
