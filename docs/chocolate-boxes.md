# Chocolate Boxes

These settings belong to the staged Chocolate endgame update; deployment requires the paired client patch.

One box gives one ordinary item. No Excellent, Ancient or socket rewards. Pink and Blue equipment is +6–+9; the two rare maces always drop +0. Luck, ADD and applicable weapon Skill use the shared server chances.

## Pink

Armor 60%, weapons 30%, shields 10%. Armor class shares below are percentages of all openings, not conditional percentages. Pieces are equally likely within a selected set. MG has no helmet; RF has no gloves.

| Class | Armor | Overall armor chance | Weapons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DW | Dark Soul | 8% | Platina Staff, Staff of Kundun |
| DK | Dark Phoenix, Great Dragon | 4% each | Bone Blade, Dark Breaker |
| Elf | Red Spirit | 8% | Great Reign Crossbow, Albatross Bow |
| MG | Hurricane | 8% | Sword Dancer, Dark Reign Blade |
| DL | Dark Master | 8% | Shining Scepter, Great Lord Scepter |
| SUM | Aura (client Storm Blitz) | 10% | Storm Blitz Stick |
| RF | Phoenix Soul | 10% | Phoenix Soul Star |

Each class receives 30/7% of all openings as weapons. Multiple weapons split that class share equally. Shields are Guardian (DW), Crimson Glory (DK), Frost Barrier (Elf), Salamander (MG), Cross (DL): 2% overall each.

## Blue

Armor 70%, ordinary weapons 25%, rare Mace of King 5% overall. Each of six classes receives 70/6% armor and 25/6% ordinary weapons. No RF armor or ordinary weapon is included.

| Class | Armor | Weapons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DW | Venom Mist | Grand Viper Staff |
| DK | Dragon Knight | Daybreak |
| Elf | Sylphid Ray | Arrow Viper Bow, Sylph Wind Bow (equal split) |
| MG | Volcano, no helmet | Explosion Blade |
| DL | Sunlight | Soleil Scepter |
| SUM | Lilium (client Eternal Wing) | Eternal Wing Stick |

SUM Lilium/Eternal Wing is one set with shared IDs, not two separate sets. Base DEF totals 268. Its STR/AGI requirements now progress above Pink while retaining zero Energy requirement. Eternal Wing Stick keeps physical 66–74 and speed 30; base wizardry rises to 65%.

## Red and sockets

Red gives Royal Mace of Emberion (the Royal Mace of King), +0, at 100% for now. Normal mace base damage is 150–165; Royal is 200–220. Both retain +4 minimum and maximum damage per upgrade level and normal jewel upgrade rules.

Selected Chocolate rewards lose only their level-380 wearing requirement; their stat/class requirements remain. Sockets are disabled globally: no new socket counts, seed crafting, socket stats, set bonuses or socket tooltips. Existing items are retained; the one-time update clears their socket counts and socket-only options while preserving ordinary options and item properties.

Star, Ribbon, Candy source-level variants, Kundun and Blood Castle reward pools are unchanged. Chocolate Boxes remain excluded from ordinary monster drops. Existing shop layout is unchanged.
