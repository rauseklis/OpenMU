# Chocolate Boxes

Pink Chocolate Box retains its existing opening rewards for now; its new reward plan has not been designed or installed.

Red Chocolate Box and Blue Chocolate Box are intentionally empty. Opening either consumes the box and gives **no item and no Zen**. They do not have a 69-Zen fallback because the existing server opening logic supports an empty result.

Chocolate Boxes are distinct from Red, Green and Blue Ribbon Boxes. Ribbon rewards and Blood Castle payouts are unchanged by these Chocolate settings. Chocolate Boxes remain excluded from ordinary monster drops in the reviewed bonus configuration.
