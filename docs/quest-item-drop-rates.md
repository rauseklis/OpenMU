# Class quest item drop chances

Items of the legacy class quests drop per monster kill. Update 172 sets the chances below in existing databases; new databases use the same values.

| Quest | Item (per class) | Monsters | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 0 Find the Scroll of Emperor (level 150) | Scroll of Emperor | any monster of level 45-60 | 0.10 % (1 in 1000) | 0.0667 % (1 in 1500) |
| 1 Treasures of MU (level 150) | Broken Sword / Tear of Elf / Soul Shard of Wizard / Eye of Abyssal | any monster of level 62-76 | 0.10 % | 0.0667 % (1 in 1500) |
| 2 Gain Hero Status (level 220) | Scroll of Emperor | any monster of level 72-108 | 0.10 % | 0.0333 % (1 in 3000) |
| 3 Secret of the Dark Stone (level 220, Blade Knight) | Broken Sword | any monster of level 78-108 | 0.10 % | 0.0333 % (1 in 3000) |
| 4 Evidence of Strength (level 380) | Flame of Death Beam Knight | Death Beam Knight | 3 % | 1.5 % |
| 4 Evidence of Strength | Horn of Hell Maine | Hell Maine | 4 % | 2 % |
| 4 Evidence of Strength | Feather of Dark Phoenix | Dark Phoenix | 5 % | 2.5 % |

Quests 5 and 6 (kill counts) and the Season 6 kill quests are unchanged.

## Expected time (first class change, quests 0 and 1)

Both pieces together need on average 3000 kills. Assumed kill speed of a level 150+ character at an area-skill farming spot: 1 kill every 2 seconds (1800 per hour) up to 1 kill every 4 seconds (900 per hour), which gives about 1 h 40 min up to 3 h 20 min on average. Lost Tower has 313 spawned monsters of level 45-60 and Atlans 219 of level 62-76, so spawn counts do not limit the kill speed. The experience rates of x5 and x500 do not change the drop chance.

The chance of a single piece within a short time stays small but not zero (random drops); the numbers are averages.
